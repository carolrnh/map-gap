Google Business Profile suspended? This report will not get it back.

A suspension is Google’s decision. You appeal to Google. We do not file it, we do not unsuspend listings, and this page is not a product.

For HVAC and plumbing shops only.

Do this

Use Google’s official help and appeal from the account that owns the listing:

Google: suspended or disabled profiles

Google Help: deceptive content (opens Google, not us).

Trade press (not a Map Gap claim): ACHR News, Jun 15, 2025, notes HVAC and plumbing as higher-risk Google Business categories.

Which problem is this?

  • Suspended / not approved / deceptive content — stay here. Appeal to Google. Stop.
  • Listing vanished or “updated” — why did my listing disappear?
  • Live, but customers don’t see you on the map — that’s not showing on Maps, not a suspension.

Don’t do this

  • Pay a reinstatement mill.
  • Pay Map Gap for a ranking report to “fix” a suspension.
  • Buy fake reviews or change the business name to game policy.

If the listing is actually live

Search Maps for the exact shop name. If the profile opens, it is not suspended. Then the public-gaps teaser on the home page is the product — not this page.
