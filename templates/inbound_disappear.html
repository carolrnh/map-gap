Why did my Google Business listing disappear?

Gone from Maps is usually one of three things. Pick the fork you can actually see. We don’t recover listings and we don’t invent a cause.

For HVAC and plumbing shops only.

1. Suspended or “not approved”

If Google says the profile is suspended, disabled, or not approved: stop. That is an appeal to Google, not a ranking report.

Suspension is Google’s appeal

2. Merged / duplicate / “this listing has been updated”

Use Google’s own listing tools in the account that owns the profile. We don’t take ownership. We don’t merge listings for you.

3. Live, but customers don’t see it
